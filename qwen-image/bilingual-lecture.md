# Qwen-Image 论文中英对照讲义

**Bilingual study guide · 中文理解 + English explanation**  
依据 Qwen Team, *Qwen-Image Technical Report*, arXiv:2508.02324v1。页码为指定PDF页码。英文为帮助学习与展示的解释性表述，并非论文逐字引文；教学算例与建议实验不代表真实模型实验。每节先中文、后英文；原有20页逐页中英讲稿与30组中英问答继续在本页提供。

## 01｜论文想解决什么？ / What problem does the paper address?

**中文讲解：** 生成一张好看的海报并不代表任务完成。店名、价格、换行或中文笔画只要出错，图片就可能无法使用。编辑已有海报时，用户也许只想改价格；如果模型连杯子或背景一起改掉，就破坏了不应改变的内容。论文聚焦两个相关但不同的问题：**复杂文字渲染**与**编辑一致性**。（§1，p.6）

**English explanation:** A visually appealing poster can still fail if the shop name, price, line breaks, or Chinese characters are incorrect. When editing an existing poster, changing the price should not unnecessarily alter the cup or the background. The paper focuses on two related but distinct challenges: **precise text rendering** and **consistent image editing**. The café poster is an illustrative teaching example, not a reported experiment.

**展示可说 / Presentation line:** “A beautiful image is not enough: the requested text must be correct, and an edit should preserve the content that the user did not ask to change.”

## 02｜整体架构 / Overall architecture

**中文讲解：** 系统由三个主要组件协作：Qwen2.5-VL 把提示词编码成条件特征，编辑时也处理源图；VAE 把图像压缩成 latent 并在最后解码回像素；MMDiT 根据当前 latent、时间和条件预测变化方向。表1所列的 **20B 是 MMDiT 主干参数**，不是整个系统全部组件的参数总和。（Fig.6 p.7；Table 1 p.9）

**English explanation:** Three main components work together. Qwen2.5-VL encodes the prompt into conditioning features and also processes the source image for editing. The VAE compresses images into latent representations and decodes the final latent back into pixels. MMDiT predicts how the current latent should change, conditioned on the input and time. The reported **20 billion parameters refer to the MMDiT backbone**, not the total number of parameters across the whole system.

**直观类比 / Analogy:** VLM 像理解需求的设计师；VAE 像压缩和解压工具；MMDiT 像逐步作画的执行者。 / The VLM interprets the request, the VAE represents the image compactly, and MMDiT progressively constructs the output. These are analogies, not literal model roles.

## 03｜VAE：细节为什么重要？ / Why does the VAE matter?

**中文讲解：** VAE 先编码图像再还原；如果细小笔画在这一表示中难以重建，生成模型的细节表达也会受到限制。作者采用 Wan-2.1-VAE 架构，冻结编码器，并用富含文字的图像微调图像解码器。文字数据上的重建PSNR从26.77提高到36.63 dB；这是一项重建指标，**不是文字准确率提升9.86%**。（§2.3 p.8；Table 2 p.20）

**English explanation:** The VAE encodes and reconstructs images. If small strokes cannot be reconstructed reliably, the latent representation can constrain downstream detail. The authors use the Wan-2.1-VAE architecture, freeze its encoder, and fine-tune the image decoder on text-rich images. The reported text-image reconstruction PSNR rises from 26.77 to 36.63 dB. PSNR measures reconstruction quality; it is **not** a percentage improvement in text-generation accuracy.

## 04｜MMDiT 和位置编码 / MMDiT and positional encoding

**中文讲解：** MMDiT 使用文字流和图像流处理不同模态，并通过注意力交互。对于“左边红色杯子、右边菜单”，模型既要理解对象属性，也要处理位置关系。MSRoPE 是论文中的多模态位置编码设计：在文本侧使用对角位置表示，在图像侧保留适合分辨率扩展的二维位置结构。编辑时再加入frame维度区分多张图像。（§2.4 pp.8–9；Fig.14 p.18）

**English explanation:** MMDiT processes text and image streams and allows them to interact through attention. For a prompt such as “a red cup on the left and a menu on the right,” the model must represent object attributes as well as spatial relationships. MSRoPE is the paper’s multimodal positional-encoding design: it represents text positions along a diagonal and image positions on a two-dimensional grid to support resolution scaling. For editing, an additional frame dimension distinguishes different images.

**证据边界 / Evidence boundary:** 设计动机和架构图说明了作者为什么这样设计；它们本身不能量化MSRoPE的独立贡献。 / The design rationale alone does not quantify the independent contribution of MSRoPE.

## 05｜数据与渐进训练 / Data and progressive training

**中文讲解：** 作者报告的数据大致分为 Nature 55%、Design 27%、People 13%、Synthetic 5%。设计类包括海报、界面和幻灯片；合成类主要指受控文字渲染与模板合成，**不指其他AI模型生成的图片**。训练逐步从一般图像引入富文字数据，并从约256p扩展到640p、1328p，同时改进筛选和数据分布。（§3 pp.9–14；§4.1.3 p.16）

**English explanation:** The reported data mixture is approximately 55% Nature, 27% Design, 13% People, and 5% Synthetic. Design data includes posters, interfaces, and slides. Here, synthetic data primarily means controlled text rendering and template composition; the authors explicitly distinguish it from images generated by other AI models. Training progressively introduces text-rich examples and increases resolution from roughly 256p to 640p and then 1328p, alongside changes to filtering and data balance.

**展示可说 / Presentation line:** “The improvement is supported by a curriculum and a text-oriented data pipeline, not simply by collecting more images.”

## 06｜Flow matching：从位置到速度 / From interpolation to velocity

**中文讲解：** 按论文 Eq.(1) 的约定，将 x₀ 理解为干净图像 latent，x₁ 为高斯噪声：xₜ = t x₀ + (1−t)x₁。t=0 时是噪声，t=1 时是干净端点。对时间 t 求导，得到目标速度 vₜ=dxₜ/dt=x₀−x₁。论文p.15前文图像和latent的符号有混用；这里沿公式明确采用latent解释。

**English explanation:** Under the convention in Eq.(1), interpret x₀ as the clean image latent and x₁ as Gaussian noise: xₜ = t x₀ + (1−t)x₁. At t=0 the state is noise; at t=1 it is the clean endpoint. Differentiating with respect to time gives the target velocity vₜ = dxₜ/dt = x₀−x₁. Other papers may use the opposite time convention, so state the convention before presenting the equation.

**算例 / Worked example:** x₀=2，x₁=−1，t=0.25 → xₜ=−0.25，目标速度 / target velocity = 3。数字为手设的一维教学例子，并非模型运行结果。 / These are hand-chosen scalar teaching values, not a model run.（Eq.1，p.15）

## 07｜损失、训练与推理 / Loss, training, and inference

**中文讲解：** 模型接收 xₜ、t 和条件 h，输出预测速度 vθ(xₜ,t,h)。论文 Eq.(2) 用预测速度与目标速度的平方误差训练模型；预测2.5、目标3时，一维平方误差为0.25。训练时有干净样本可构造目标；推理时没有预先知道的目标图，系统从噪声出发，反复预测速度并进行数值积分。简单欧拉一步可写为 x(t+Δt)≈x(t)+Δt·vθ(x(t),t,h)。真实速度会随状态、时间和条件变化。（Eq.1–2 p.15）

**English explanation:** Given xₜ, time t, and conditioning h, the model predicts a velocity vθ(xₜ,t,h). Equation (2) trains it by minimizing the squared error between the predicted and target velocities. If the prediction is 2.5 and the target is 3, the one-dimensional squared error is 0.25. During training, a clean sample is available to construct the target. During inference, the target image is not known in advance: generation starts from noise and numerically integrates the learned velocity field. A simplified Euler step is x(t+Δt)≈x(t)+Δt·vθ(x(t),t,h); the real predicted field depends on the current state, time, and conditioning.

**演讲可说 / Presentation line:** “Training uses a clean sample to define the supervision target; inference starts from noise and uses the learned field without seeing the target image.”

## 08｜双路编辑与多任务 / Dual-conditioned editing and multi-task training

**中文讲解：** 编辑时，同一源图走两条条件路径：Qwen2.5-VL与文字指令一起提供高层语义，VAE编码提供有助于保持细节的图像 latent。两路信息和目标图的加噪 latent 一起进入 MMDiT。训练中的目标图与作为条件的源图角色不同。论文还结合T2I、I2I重建与TI2I编辑；架构动机合理，但各路径的独立效果仍需控制变量的消融实验。（Fig.14、§4.3，pp.18–19）

**English explanation:** In editing, the same source image provides two types of conditioning. Qwen2.5-VL combines the source image and instruction into high-level semantic features, while the VAE produces image latents that help preserve visual detail. Both conditions are used by MMDiT with the noised target latent. The target image used during training has a different role from the source image used as a condition. The system also combines text-to-image generation, image reconstruction, and text-and-image-to-image editing. The design motivates complementary roles, but controlled ablations are needed to isolate each route’s contribution.

## 09｜SFT、DPO 与 GRPO / Post-training

**中文讲解：** SFT使用经过筛选和标注的高质量样本继续训练。论文中的DPO利用人类选出的较好和较差结果、结合参考模型进行偏好优化；GRPO利用同一提示下候选结果的组内相对奖励，并在完整目标中包含裁剪概率比与KL项。论文报告先做较大规模DPO，再做较小规模、较细粒度的GRPO。（§4.2，pp.16–18）

**English explanation:** Supervised fine-tuning continues training on selected and annotated high-quality examples. In this report, DPO uses human-selected preferred and rejected outputs together with a reference model. GRPO compares rewards among candidates generated for the same prompt; its full objective also includes a clipped policy ratio and a KL term. The report describes larger-scale DPO followed by smaller, fine-grained GRPO refinement.

## 10｜GRPO 数学示例 / A worked GRPO example

**中文讲解：** 论文 Eq.(4) 的相对优势为 Aᵢ=(rᵢ−组内平均奖励)/组内奖励标准差。自拟奖励[2,4,6]的均值为4；若教学例子采用总体标准差，σ=√(8/3)≈1.633，优势约为[−1.225,0,1.225]。正值只说明高于该组平均，并不保证绝对质量高。奖励全相同时标准差为0，公式直接归一化无定义；论文该式未明确给出具体处理约定。（Eq.4，p.17）

**English explanation:** Equation (4) defines a relative advantage as Aᵢ=(rᵢ−group mean)/group standard deviation. For the illustrative rewards [2,4,6], the mean is 4. If this teaching example uses the **population** standard deviation, σ=√(8/3)≈1.633 and the advantages are approximately [−1.225,0,1.225]. A positive advantage means above the group average; it does not establish that the output is good in absolute terms. If all rewards are equal, the standard deviation is zero and direct normalization is undefined. The report does not specify the implementation’s exact zero-variance handling at this equation.

## 12｜GEdit 几何均值 / The GEdit geometric mean

**中文讲解：** 对每个样本先计算 √(语义一致性 × 感知质量)，再对样本求平均。举两个自拟样本：(9,1) 和 (1,9)，逐样本几何均值分别为3，平均为3。若错误地先对语义和质量分别求平均，再开方，得到√(5×5)=5。两个结果不同，因此计算顺序不能省略。这里的两个样本是教学例子。（Table 11，p.24）

**English explanation:** First compute √(semantic consistency × perceptual quality) for each sample, then average those per-sample values. For two illustrative samples (9,1) and (1,9), both per-sample geometric means are 3, so their average is 3. If one instead averages each component first and takes the geometric mean, the result is √(5×5)=5. The results differ; therefore, the order of aggregation matters. These numbers are a teaching example, not paper measurements.

## 13｜批判性讨论 / Critical discussion

**中文讲解：** 论文的价值在于把文字和编辑一致性作为明确目标，并综合数据、模型与训练策略。但同时改变多种组件后，最终结果不能自动归因给某一个设计。部分编辑基准使用模型评委；微小差距、评价偏差、训练数据与算力披露也影响结论和复现。作者对统一视觉理解与生成的展望，不等于本文已证明模型具备完整视觉理解。（§§3–6，pp.9–39）

**English explanation:** The report’s strength is that it treats accurate text and consistent editing as explicit objectives and combines data, model, and training choices to address them. However, when several components change together, the final result cannot automatically be attributed to a single design. Model-based evaluators, small score margins, and limited disclosure of training data or compute also affect interpretation and reproducibility. The authors’ vision of unified visual understanding and generation should not be confused with a demonstrated solution to every aspect of visual understanding.

**建议实验 / Proposed experiment:** 固定数据、算力和主干，比较仅语义、仅VAE、双路条件；分开衡量指令遵循与未修改区域保持，并使用多次随机种子和人工评价。 / Hold data, compute, and backbone fixed; compare semantic-only, VAE-only, and dual conditioning; separately measure instruction adherence and preservation, using repeated seeds and human evaluation. This is a proposal, not an experiment reported as completed.
