数据、渐进训练与VAEData, progressive training and the VAE

受控文字合成与富文字图像提供训练覆盖；训练逐步提高难度和分辨率。VAE冻结编码器、微调解码器以保留笔画，重建PSNR从26.77升至36.63 dB；这不是文字生成准确率。

  • Text-rich images and rendered text; progressive resolution: 256p → 640p → 1328p.
  • Freeze the VAE encoder; tune the decoder to preserve small strokes.
  • Text reconstruction PSNR: 26.77 → 36.63 dB; not prompted-text accuracy.

Flow matching：公式、算例与推理Flow matching: formula, example and inference

x₀为干净latent，x₁为噪声；t=0对应噪声，t=1对应干净端。对插值求导得到目标速度。训练用已知干净样本构造监督；推理从噪声出发积分，不知道目标图像。

  • Differentiate the interpolation to obtain target velocity x₀−x₁.
  • Worked scalar example: x₀=2, x₁=−1, t=0.25 gives xₜ=−0.25.
  • Inference integrates a learned velocity field without access to the clean endpoint.
xₜ = t x₀ + (1−t)x₁
v* = dxₜ/dt = x₀−x₁
L = E[‖vθ(xₜ,t,h)−v*‖²]
x₀=2, x₁=−1, t=0.25 → xₜ=−0.25; v*=3; (2.5−3)²=0.25
推理更新与概念图Inference update and conceptual illustration

x(t+Δt) ≈ x(t)+Δt·vθ(x(t),t,h)

简化欧拉说明；真实速度随状态、时间和条件变化。A simplified Euler explanation; real velocity depends on state, time and conditions.

来源Source · Report §4.1, p.15, Eqs.(1)–(2) ↗
精简讲稿与中文理解Concise speaker notes +
xₜ = t x₀ + (1−t)x₁; t=0 is noise. Differentiate: dxₜ/dt = x₀−x₁. Learn vθ(xₜ,t,h) by minimizing squared velocity error. State the time convention before differentiating. The derivative of the straight interpolation is the difference between its endpoints. The neural network learns a conditional velocity field from many image, noise and time samples.
x₀为干净latent、x₁为噪声。本论文t=0为噪声，t=1为干净端；对插值求导得到目标速度x₀−x₁，模型用平方误差学习。

后训练：SFT、DPO、GRPOPost-training: SFT, DPO and GRPO

SFT使用高质量样本；DPO学习偏好对；GRPO利用同一prompt下候选的组内相对奖励。正优势仅代表高于组均值；完整目标还包含裁剪与KL。

  • SFT: curated examples; DPO: preferred/rejected pairs.
  • GRPO: group-relative rewards, clipping and KL regularization.
  • Positive advantage is relative, not proof of absolute quality.
Aᵢ = (rᵢ−μ) / σ
r=[2,4,6], μ=4, σ=√(8/3) → A≈[−1.225,0,1.225]

示例采用总体标准差；全奖励相同时σ=0，直接归一化未定义。完整GRPO还含裁剪与KL。Population standard deviation is assumed here. Equal rewards give σ=0; direct normalization is undefined. Full GRPO also includes clipping and KL.

来源Source · Report §§4.2.1–4.2.2 pp.16–18; Table 4 p.21 ↗
精简讲稿与中文理解Concise speaker notes +
SFT: curated examples; DPO: preferred/rejected pairs. GRPO: group-relative rewards, clipping and KL regularization. Positive advantage is relative, not proof of absolute quality. For illustrative rewards two, four and six, the group mean is four. A population-standard-deviation convention gives advantages around minus one point two-two-five, zero and plus one point two-two-five. Equal rewards create zero variance; the displayed normalization needs an implementation convention.
SFT使用高质量样本；DPO学习偏好对；GRPO利用同一prompt下候选的组内相对奖励。正优势仅代表高于组均值；完整目标还包含裁剪与KL。
